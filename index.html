What is a
Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a shopping comparison sheet used to organize product links, category labels, prices, sizes and review notes. A useful sheet keeps the source and date beside each entry so you can check information before ordering. This page provides a discovery guide, rather than a live inventory feed or downloadable sheet.

03 / From discovery to decision

A shortlist
with a method.

Use this Litbuy spreadsheet guide to turn scattered product links into a comparison you can revisit.

Find your starting point ↗
  1. 01

    Choose one category first

    Start with sneakers, a clothing layer or an accessory. Save the source link and keep different product types easy to filter.

  2. 02

    Record the same fields for every find

    Capture the model, color, measurements, price, currency and date checked. Mark missing details as unknown instead of filling them with guesses.

  3. 03

    Compare evidence, not just presentation

    Review seller information, listing photographs, available inspection images and return terms. Keep a separate column for questions still unanswered.

  4. 04

    Check the complete cost before deciding

    Include delivery, service charges and applicable import costs. Recheck the destination listing before purchasing because prices and availability can change.

05 / Quick answers

A little clarity
before you browse.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a shopping comparison sheet used to organize product links, category labels, prices, sizes and review notes. Its usefulness depends on the accuracy and freshness of the information entered.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and a comparison guide. Its shopping links open CAFANS; it does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet or a live Litbuy inventory feed.

Can I compare shoes, clothing and accessories in one sheet?

Yes. Use a shared set of fields for source links, prices and dates, then add category-specific fields such as shoe length, garment measurements, hat circumference or bracelet dimensions.

Do the reference images confirm stock or authenticity?

No. The images are visual references. They do not confirm CAFANS stock, seller authorization, authenticity or a relationship between the brands and this guide.

How should I compare the total shopping cost?

Compare the item price plus domestic shipping, service fees, international shipping and applicable import charges. Keep the currency and date checked beside each estimate.

Is this an official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery and comparison guide. It is not operated by or affiliated with Litbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.
